Our Story

From a tiny studio to 50,000+ fans

ODDAWURLD started in 2018 in a small San Francisco studio with one obsession: technology should feel as good as it performs. Frustrated by gadgets that looked cheap or broke too soon, our founders began hand-testing every device before it reached a customer.

Today we ship to more than 120 countries, partner with world-class engineers and still test every product in-house — because premium is not a price, it is a promise.
